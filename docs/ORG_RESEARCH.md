This file is a template. **Only team members who actually spoke with an organization fill in answers.** Never

If real answers are recorded here, the seed can gain a variant based on them, labeled "based on a real interview".

## 10-minute call script

1. When can you receive donated food? Days, hours, and how late is too late?
2. Can someone meet a delivery at the curb, including a self-driving car or robot?
3. Do you take hot prepared food? How quickly must it arrive, and can you hold it hot?
4. What cold, frozen or shelf-stable food can you store, and how much?
5. Any dietary or allergen rules?
6. What records do you need for each donation, and who do you report to?
7. How often do you need reports, and in what format?
8. What has gone wrong with food donations before?
